# Art Style Guidelines

## Theme
Soviet-era World War II military hospital. Bleak, intimate, eerie, yet occasionally warm.

## Color Palette
- Muted palette: institutional greens, browns, grays, off-whites.
- Dirty snow and cold blues for exterior visibility through windows.
- Warm, harsh, or dim lighting for interiors depending on room (e.g., warm oil lamp or harsh electric bulb).

## Resolution & Style
- Low-resolution pixel art. Strong silhouettes.
- Ensure expressive animation, especially for characters (breathing, shivering).
- UI elements must look diegetic: paper notes, clipboards, worn photographs.
- Avoid excessive noise; clean pixel placements with clear lighting gradients.
